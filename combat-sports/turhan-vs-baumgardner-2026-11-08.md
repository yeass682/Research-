**Turhan's opposition:** Her early opposition was weak: seven of her first nine fights were in the Balkans and Turkey, and the quick knockouts came against very low-level opponents. Her last five fights are the real evidence:
- **TKO 1 Shauna Browne** (5-1), Mar 2025, a WBC eliminator taken on ten days' notice. Ended by a left hook.
- **TKO 6 Rima Ayadi**, Aug 2025, won the WBA interim 130 title.
- **TKO 5 Beatriz Ferreira**, Dec 2025, won the IBF 135 title. **Her best win.** Ferreira is an elite former amateur (Olympic medallist and amateur world champion) and was the IBF champion. Stopping a skilled, high-level boxer is the strongest sign Turhan's power works at the top level.
- **SD 10 Taylah Gentzen**, Jan 2026 (98-92, 96-94 Turhan; 97-93 Gentzen). **A warning sign:** one judge thought she lost to a boxer she was expected to beat clearly.
- **UD 10 Gabriela Tellez**, Jun 2026.

She was named 2025 Women's Fighter of the Year by BoxingScene and The Sporting News.

**Baumgardner's opposition:** Her opposition is much stronger: she unified all four titles at 130.
- **TKO 4 Terri Harper** (2021), won the WBC title. Female Knockout of the Year.
- **SD Mikaela Mayer** (2022), close.
- **UD wins** over Elhem Mekhaled, Christina Linardatou (avenging her only loss, a 2018 SD), Jennifer Miranda, Leila Beaudoin (12 rounds) and Bo Mi Re Shin.
- **Power has dropped off:** she has not stopped anyone since Harper five years ago; her last seven wins are all decisions. Six of her 7 KOs came in her first 10 fights.
- **Drug test:** a positive test in July 2023 (the WBC found no intentional use) cost her about a year. Her comeback against Delfine Persoon (Sept 2024) ended as a no contest in round 4 after a head clash.

**What the records hide:** Turhan's 14-0 with 8 KOs exaggerates her standing, because of the weak early opposition. The Ferreira stoppage, though, is a high-level result. Baumgardner's 18-1 with 7 KOs makes her look like more of a puncher than she has been lately; she now wins with skill and work rate, not stoppages.

## Style Matchup

**What the fight is about:** Baumgardner's skill against Turhan's right hand.

- **Turhan:** a heavy puncher, described as an "unorthodox, battle-tested brawler." Her **right hand is her best weapon**, often thrown "without much setup." She is also dangerous with the left hook (the Browne finish). Her weakness is distance: she reaches and lunges, so she is off balance and open to counters when she misses.
- **Baumgardner:** quick hands, strong and a good boxer. Recently she has controlled fights with her jab and is very active. But she likes to sit in the pocket and is called "one of the most hittable champions," with occasional defensive lapses.
- **How they fit together:** Turhan's lunges should give Baumgardner, the faster and more accurate fighter, plenty of chances to counter. If Baumgardner boxes behind the jab, steps off the line and counters as Turhan reaches, she should win most rounds clearly. **The danger** is her habit of sitting in the pocket. Against an opponent who knocked out an elite boxer like Ferreira, every clean right hand Baumgardner takes while standing in range could end the fight.
- **Weight:** At 130, Baumgardner's strength was an advantage. At 135 she gives up that edge, and Turhan (who has fought up to 140) will hit harder than anyone she has faced. Baumgardner has never been stopped, but she hasn't been tested by a lightweight puncher.
- **Three-minute rounds:** This is the most underrated factor. Each round gives Turhan 50% more time to land her right hand, and asks Baumgardner to stay alert for longer. Longer rounds also help a fitter, busier boxer build big rounds on the scorecards, which cuts both ways.
- **Stance:** Both are orthodox, so neither has the awkwardness of facing a southpaw.

**Who is more likely to get the fight she wants?** Baumgardner, for most of the fight. Turhan only needs a few moments.

## Physical & Current Factors

- **Weight move:** This is Baumgardner's first fight at 135, and the jump is in Turhan's favour: Turhan has been at 135 for her last three fights.
- **Activity:** Both fought within the last 5–7 months, so neither should be rusty.
- **Age:** About the same (31 and 32).
- **Home advantage:** Baumgardner lives in Dallas, close to Arlington, and is the established US star. That probably helps her with the crowd and in close rounds. Ali vs Kreps on this same card showed this commission's judges can disagree sharply.
- **Durability:** Baumgardner has never been stopped. Turhan has not been tested against an elite counter-puncher at 135.

## Key Evidence (most comparable first)

1. **Turhan TKO 5 Ferreira:** Turhan's power stopped an elite technical boxer. This is the best evidence for an upset.
2. **Turhan SD Gentzen:** When Turhan can't land the big shot, a disciplined boxer can make it close or beat her. This is the best evidence for Baumgardner.
3. **Baumgardner SD Mayer:** Against an elite, busy boxer, Baumgardner won a close fight rather than dominating. She doesn't separate herself easily from high-level opponents.
4. **Baumgardner's 130-lb title defences (Mekhaled, Linardatou, Miranda, Beaudoin, Shin):** She has repeatedly won rounds with her jab and work rate over 10–12 rounds, but at 130 and against less dangerous punchers.

## Path to Victory — Alycia Baumgardner

- **Main route:** Box behind the jab, keep Turhan reaching, counter her misses (right hand over the top, left hook as she lunges in), keep her feet moving, and win clear rounds. **UD.**
- **Second route:** A late stoppage, if Turhan tires from missing in three-minute rounds and Baumgardner's counters build up damage.
- **What has to happen:** She has to be disciplined. No long stretches standing still in the pocket, and the chin down when Turhan sets her feet.
- **What could go wrong:** Being caught by a clean right hand at 135 while standing in range.

## Path to Victory — Elif Nur Turhan

- **Main route:** Land one or two big right hands. Walk Baumgardner down, take risks in exchanges and catch her when she plants her feet in the pocket. **KO/TKO, probably in the middle or late rounds** as Turhan finds the timing.
- **Second route:** Turn it into a messy, physical fight where her power wins rounds. Unlikely to work on the cards against Baumgardner's work rate.
- **What has to happen:** She has to set up the right hand behind feints or a body attack, instead of lunging and getting countered.
- **What could go wrong:** Missing all night and losing round after round on the scorecards (as in the Gentzen fight, only worse).

## Prediction

- **Most likely winner:** Baumgardner, about **70–75%**. The betting market is higher (about 85%, at -550 to -600). I discount that because Baumgardner is hittable, she is moving up, the rounds are three minutes, and Turhan's Ferreira win showed what she can do.
- **Most likely method:** Baumgardner by unanimous decision with fairly wide cards (about 55–60%). A Baumgardner stoppage is possible but less likely (about 10–15%).
- **How Turhan pulls the upset:** Stoppage, about **20–25%**. A Turhan decision win is unlikely (about 5%).

## Confidence / Main Uncertainty

**Confidence: medium.**
- **Biggest unknown:** how well Baumgardner takes a lightweight puncher's shots. She has never been stopped, but she has never faced this level of power.
- **Turhan's level is hard to judge:** she has only a handful of fights against strong opposition. Ferreira and Gentzen point in opposite directions.
- **Gaps in the research:** official weigh-in numbers and fight-week quotes were not found, and Turhan's height and reach are not listed.
