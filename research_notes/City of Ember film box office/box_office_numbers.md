# City of Ember (2008) — Box Office Numbers: Budget, Domestic Run, International, Worldwide, Home Video

Research note on method: Wayback Machine (web.archive.org) could not be fetched in this session (tool blocked), and Deadline's 2008 article redirects to a paywall/bot gate (tollbit) that was not bypassed. Variety, Hollywood Reporter, LA Times, NYT and AP weekend wires from Oct 2008 did not surface in searches. As a result, most hard figures below come from **retrospective databases** (Box Office Mojo and The Numbers, as they stand in 2026). The **contemporaneous** (Oct 2008–Jan 2009) material found is: the Box Office Guru preview (Oct 9, 2008), TicketNews and FilmBook weekend wrap-ups (Oct 12–13, 2008), and Deadline Hollywood Daily's weekend report (Oct 2008, known only through search-engine summaries, not read directly). Each item is labeled [CONTEMPORANEOUS] or [RETROSPECTIVE].

## 1. US release, opening weekend, competition, and how it was described at the time

### Takeaway
City of Ember opened Friday, October 10, 2008, at 2,022 theaters. Final actuals put it at #11 with $3,129,473, a $1,547–$1,548 per-theater average. Sunday studio estimates had it at about $3.2M and #10, and that is how the 2008 wrap-ups reported it. It took roughly half of the ~$6M that Box Office Guru had projected. Deadline Hollywood Daily called it a costly disappointment at the time.

### Cited Findings
- **Release date:** US wide release Friday, Oct 10, 2008, by 20th Century Fox [RETROSPECTIVE] — [The Numbers](https://www.the-numbers.com/movie/City-of-Ember); [Box Office Mojo title page](https://www.boxofficemojo.com/title/tt0970411/). BOM lists an earlier opening on Thu Oct 9, 2008 in Puerto Rico — [Box Office Mojo](https://www.boxofficemojo.com/title/tt0970411/).
- **Distribution:** The film went out under the Fox-Walden joint venture, with 20th Century Fox handling distribution. Summit Entertainment handled international distribution and sales — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_Ember), which cites Allan Hunter, Screen International, Oct 10, 2008 ([Screen Daily](https://www.screendaily.com/city-of-ember/4041274.article)); [Bomb Report](https://bombreport.com/yearly-breakdowns/2008-2/city-of-ember/) [RETROSPECTIVE, 2015/2019] says "Summit sold the overseas rights".
- **Premiere:** New York premiere Oct 7, 2008 at AMC Loews 19th Street — [AceShowbiz](https://www.aceshowbiz.com/news/view/00018899.html) [CONTEMPORANEOUS]. There was also a European premiere in Belfast — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_Ember).
- **Opening weekend actuals (Oct 10–12, 2008):** #11, $3,129,473, 2,022 theaters, $1,547 average (BOM) / $1,548 (The Numbers) — [Box Office Mojo weekend 2008W41](https://www.boxofficemojo.com/weekend/2008W41/); [The Numbers weekend chart Oct 10, 2008](https://www.the-numbers.com/box-office-chart/weekend/2008/10/10) [RETROSPECTIVE databases recording 2008 actuals].
- **Weekend competition (BOM actuals, Oct 10–12, 2008):** [Box Office Mojo 2008W41](https://www.boxofficemojo.com/weekend/2008W41/)
  - #1 Beverly Hills Chihuahua (Disney), 2nd weekend: $17,502,077 at 3,218 theaters, total $52.5M
  - #2 Quarantine (Screen Gems), opening: $14,211,321 at 2,461 theaters
  - #3 Body of Lies (Warner Bros.), opening: $12,884,416 at 2,710 theaters
  - #4 Eagle Eye (DreamWorks), 3rd weekend: $10,913,762
  - #5 Nick and Norah's Infinite Playlist: $6,420,474
  - #6 The Express (Universal), opening: $4,562,675 at 2,808 theaters
  - #7 Nights in Rodanthe $4,541,201; #8 Appaloosa $3,321,389; #9 The Duchess $3,304,841; #10 Fireproof $3,140,997; **#11 City of Ember $3,129,473**; #12 Lakeview Terrace $2,654,644.
  - Of the four wide openers that weekend (Quarantine, Body of Lies, The Express, City of Ember), Ember had the fewest theaters and the lowest gross and average.
- **Pre-release projection:** Gitesh Pandya (Box Office Guru, on Rotten Tomatoes) wrote on Oct 9, 2008: "Entering the fewest locations among the new releases with 2,023 playdates, City of Ember could collect about $6M this weekend." He also wrote "Ember might connect with a core fan following, but overall it will be a tough sell" and "Plus the marketing push has not been too forceful." He noted that the Robbins/fantasy combination "didn't work so well two years ago for the effects-heavy Zathura" and that "in the post-Hobbit world most fantasy films have struggled to find audiences in the U.S." — [RT Box Office Guru Preview](https://editorial.rottentomatoes.com/article/box-office-guru-preview-audiences-surrounded-by-lies/) [CONTEMPORANEOUS; page metadata shows an updated date of 2015, but the content is the Oct 2008 preview].
- **TicketNews weekend wrap (week ending Oct 12, 2008):** "Two other films premiering this weekend reported depressed ticket sales." ... "'City of Ember' came in 10th with $3.2 million." — [TicketNews](https://www.ticketnews.com/2008/10/beverly-hills-chihuahua-remains-the-alpha-dog-at-the-box-office/) [CONTEMPORANEOUS; based on Sunday estimates].
- **FilmBook wrap (Oct 12, 2008, citing Box Office Mojo):** "The Duchess and City of Ember rounded out the top ten respectively." — [FilmBook](https://film-book.com/box-office-weekend-of-oct-10-%E2%80%93-12-2008/) [CONTEMPORANEOUS, Sunday estimates].
- **Backseat Producers wrap (Oct 13, 2008)** listed the opening as $3,200,000 — [Backseat Producers](http://backseatproducers.com/2008/10/13/weekend-box-office-for-october-10-12/) [CONTEMPORANEOUS, estimates; from search snippet only].
- **Deadline Hollywood Daily (Nikki Finke), Oct 2008.** Headline: "'BH Chihuahua' Wins Box Office Dogfight; 'Quarantine' Takes #2; DiCaprio/Crowe's Costly 'Body Of Lies' Needs A Body Bag." According to search-engine summaries, the article reported City of Ember at #10 with ~$3.2M from 2,022 theaters (about $970K Friday, $1.2M Saturday). It described the Fox Walden/Bill Murray film as weak despite its high cost, a costly flop meant to launch a family franchise — [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2008/10/dicaprio-crowe-race-dog-pic-for-friday-no-1-7231/) [CONTEMPORANEOUS, but **NOT READ DIRECTLY**: paywall redirect. The wording is the search tool's paraphrase, not a verbatim quote, and needs verification before quoting].
- **The Numbers' contemporaneous-style weekend note** (per a search snippet): the new releases "were not able to match modest expectations," and the overall box office slipped 2% to about $100M versus the prior weekend — [The Numbers weekend chart Oct 10, 2008](https://www.the-numbers.com/box-office-chart/weekend/2008/10/10). When the page was fetched directly it showed only the data chart, so this wording is unconfirmed.
- **Critical reception context:** Variety's Justin Chang review was headlined "Eye-popping 'Ember' burns out" (Variety, dated Oct 13, 2008 per Wikipedia). Stephen Holden, NYT, Oct 10, 2008, said Saoirse Ronan's talents were "wasted." The CinemaScore was "B+" — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_Ember).

### Inferences
- The 2008 reports said "#10 / $3.2M" and the databases say "#11 / $3.13M" because the first relied on Sunday estimates and the second on Monday actuals. On actuals, Ember finished just $11,524 behind #10 Fireproof ($3,140,997), so the estimate-based #10 flipped to #11.
- BOM's opening average ($1,547) and The Numbers' ($1,548) differ only by rounding.
- The Box Office Guru preview gave 2,023 theaters; the actual opening count was 2,022. The 2,023 figure matches the widest count reached in weekend 2.

### Gaps
- Box Office Mojo's own weekend report by Brandon Gray (Oct 12–13, 2008) was not found. Old BOM news pages and Wayback snapshots could not be fetched.
- No Variety, Hollywood Reporter, LA Times, NYT or AP weekend box office story from Oct 12–13, 2008 was retrieved. Their characterizations of the opening are unverified here.
- Deadline's exact wording is unverified (paraphrase only).

## 2. Weekend-by-weekend run, theater loss, days in release, final domestic

### Takeaway
The film fell 45% in weekend 2, then lost 73% of its theaters going into weekend 3 (2,023 to 536) and dropped 72%. It was out of the top 20 by its third weekend and ran 14 tracked weekends, through Jan 9–11, 2009. Final domestic gross was $7,873,007, so the opening was 39.7% of the total (2.52x multiplier).

### Inferences
- Theater count fell 73.5% between weekends 2 and 3 (2,023 to 536), which points to Fox pulling the film quickly. Bomb Report's 2015 retrospective says "FOX dumped the film," but that is opinion.
- The cume after weekend 14 ($7,871,693) is $1,314 short of the final $7,873,007, so the film earned a few more weekdays and/or small grosses after Jan 11, 2009. The run lasted at least ~94 days (Oct 10, 2008 to Jan 11, 2009). BOM's exact "days in release" or close date was not retrieved.
- About 89% of the domestic total ($7.04M of $7.87M) came in October 2008.

### Gaps
- The exact close date and total days in release could not be confirmed. BOM's release page returned HTTP 400, and Wayback was not accessible.

## 3. Production budget and marketing/P&A

### Takeaway
Sources split between **$55 million** (Box Office Mojo, Wikipedia, IMDb, Bomb Report) and **$38 million** (The Numbers). No 2008 trade source stating either figure was found, so both are retrospective database figures here. No P&A figure was found. There is only anecdotal evidence of costly promotion, such as Walden's Comic-Con train junket, and of Fox absorbing Walden's marketing unit just before release.

### Cited Findings
- $55,000,000 budget — [Box Office Mojo](https://www.boxofficemojo.com/title/tt0970411/) [RETROSPECTIVE]; [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_Ember) (cites BOM as retrieved June 9, 2009, so BOM likely carried $55M by mid-2009); [IMDb FAQ](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0970411/faq/) ("$55,000,000 (estimated)").
- $38,000,000 production budget — [The Numbers](https://www.the-numbers.com/movie/City-of-Ember) [RETROSPECTIVE]. **This contradicts BOM's $55M.**
- "The budget for City Of Ember was $55 million and it was financed by Walden Media." Playtone bought the novel rights in Oct 2004 for "mid-six figures," and Walden and Fox formed their partnership in Aug 2006 — [Bomb Report](https://bombreport.com/yearly-breakdowns/2008-2/city-of-ember/) [RETROSPECTIVE, 2015, unsourced].
- Marketing: "Walden spent lavishly on promoting the movie at Comic Con." It rented two vintage 1949 train cars attached to an Amtrak train for about two dozen reporters and bloggers. "Just one week before City Of Ember's release, FOX absorbed the Walden marketing branch, laid off 12 employees" — [Bomb Report](https://bombreport.com/yearly-breakdowns/2008-2/city-of-ember/) [RETROSPECTIVE, unsourced]; also [Bomb Report on X, Oct 10, 2018](https://twitter.com/bombreport/status/1050035343293120513).
- Box Office Guru, Oct 9, 2008: "the marketing push has not been too forceful" — [RT](https://editorial.rottentomatoes.com/article/box-office-guru-preview-audiences-surrounded-by-lies/) [CONTEMPORANEOUS].
- Deadline (Oct 2008) reportedly stressed the film's "high cost" — [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2008/10/dicaprio-crowe-race-dog-pic-for-friday-no-1-7231/) [CONTEMPORANEOUS, paraphrase only; no dollar figure confirmed].

### Inferences
- Wikipedia's citation suggests the $55M figure was on BOM by 2009, which makes it the older and more widely repeated number. Its original source (studio, trade or BOM estimate) is unknown.

### Gaps
- No 2008 trade article (Variety, THR, LA Times) stating the budget was found.
- No P&A or marketing spend figure was found from any source.
- The Fox absorption of Walden marketing and the layoffs were likely reported in Variety or THR around Oct 2008, but this could not be verified.

## 4. International release by territory, international total, worldwide total

### Takeaway
BOM records about **$10.06M international** and **$17.93M worldwide**. The Numbers records **$9.96M international** and **$17.83M worldwide**. The $98,126 gap in both cases is exactly BOM's Puerto Rico gross. The UK was the largest foreign market ($2.47M), opening day-and-date with the US on Oct 10, 2008. The rollout was staggered through July 2009.

- Worldwide gross was about $17.9M against a $55M budget (about 33%) or a $38M budget (about 47%). On either figure the theatrical run recouped well under the production cost, before counting distributor rental share and P&A.
- The UK alone provided about 25% of the international gross. Western Europe (UK, Italy, Spain, France) together accounted for about $6.5M, roughly 64% of international.

## 5. Home video (DVD/Blu-ray) release and sales

### Takeaway
The US DVD came out **January 20, 2009** from Fox Home Entertainment. The Numbers estimates domestic DVD sales at **$8,381,241**, slightly more than the film's domestic theatrical gross. The first reported week was about 105,225 units ($2.1M), ranked #9. There was no US Blu-ray; Blu-rays came out in the UK, Australia, Germany and France in 2009.

### Cited Findings
- US DVD release Jan 20, 2009 by Fox Home Entertainment — [The Numbers](https://www.the-numbers.com/movie/City-of-Ember); [DVDsReleaseDates](https://www.dvdsreleasedates.com/movies/3950/City-of-Ember-(2008).html). It was a DVD-18 (double-sided widescreen/full-screen) disc — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_Ember) (uncited there).
- Estimated domestic DVD sales total: $8,381,241. No Blu-ray figure is listed — [The Numbers](https://www.the-numbers.com/movie/City-of-Ember) [RETROSPECTIVE estimate; The Numbers' video data derives from industry tracking].
- First-week video chart row (week ending Jan 25, 2009): rank 9, 105,225 units, $2,103,448 consumer spending — [The Numbers (mobile)](https://m.the-numbers.com/movie/City-of-Ember). This comes from a search snippet only; the page could not be fetched to confirm.
- "FOX never even bothered to release the film on Blu-Ray in the US and stripped all extras off the DVD" — [Bomb Report](https://bombreport.com/yearly-breakdowns/2008-2/city-of-ember/) [RETROSPECTIVE]. Wikipedia lists 2009 Region B Blu-rays (UK, Australia, Germany, France) and a 2010 Hong Kong Region A Blu-ray — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_Ember) (uncited).
- 2009 DVD reviews referred to the film's "dismal" ~$7.9M domestic gross — [DVD Talk](https://www.dvdtalk.com/reviews/35993/city-of-ember/); [Inside Pulse, Jan 31, 2009](https://insidepulse.com/2009/01/31/city-of-ember-dvd-review/) [CONTEMPORANEOUS reviews; wording from search summary].
- UK: an Official Charts DVD chart entry, possibly #34, in a chart dated around March 1, 2009 — [Official Charts](https://www.officialcharts.com/charts/dvd-chart/20090301/141/) (from a search snippet; position and date not confirmed).

### Inferences
- Estimated DVD sales (~$8.4M) were slightly above the domestic box office (~$7.9M). Home video was proportionally significant, but it did not come close to closing the gap with the budget.

### Gaps
- Nielsen VideoScan, Home Media Magazine and Video Business reports from Jan–Feb 2009 were not found. Weekly unit totals beyond the first week and total units are unverified.
- No figures were found for international home video, TV or ancillary revenue.

## 6. Did 2008 coverage call it a flop, bomb, or disappointment?

### Takeaway
Yes, but the evidence found is thin and mostly paraphrased. Deadline Hollywood Daily (Oct 2008) reportedly framed it as a weak, costly underperformer. TicketNews (Oct 2008) said it "reported depressed ticket sales." Box Office Guru had predicted a "tough sell" beforehand. The explicit "box-office bomb" label appears mainly in retrospective sources, especially Wikipedia and Bomb Report.

### Cited Findings
- TicketNews, week ending Oct 12, 2008: "Two other films premiering this weekend [City of Ember and The Express] reported depressed ticket sales." — [TicketNews](https://www.ticketnews.com/2008/10/beverly-hills-chihuahua-remains-the-alpha-dog-at-the-box-office/) [CONTEMPORANEOUS, verbatim].
- Box Office Guru (Gitesh Pandya), Oct 9, 2008: "overall it will be a tough sell" — [RT](https://editorial.rottentomatoes.com/article/box-office-guru-preview-audiences-surrounded-by-lies/) [CONTEMPORANEOUS, verbatim; pre-release].
- Deadline Hollywood Daily, Oct 2008: weak #10 debut "despite its high cost," a costly would-be family franchise launch — [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2008/10/dicaprio-crowe-race-dog-pic-for-friday-no-1-7231/) [CONTEMPORANEOUS, **paraphrase from search summaries; verify before quoting**].
- Variety review headline: "Eye-popping 'Ember' burns out" (Justin Chang, Oct 2008). This is a critical verdict, not a box office one — cited in [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_Ember).
- Retrospective: Wikipedia calls it "a box-office bomb," grossing $17.9M "well below" its $55M budget — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_Ember). Moria Reviews calls it the least financially successful Walden Media film (and gives a conflicting $3.5M opening) — [Moria](https://moriareviews.com/sciencefiction/city-of-ember-2008.htm). IMDb trivia says the failure caused the sequel adaptation to be shelved — [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0970411/faq/) (from a search summary).

### Inferences
- Contemporaneous coverage treated Ember as a minor footnote to a weekend dominated by Chihuahua, Quarantine and Body of Lies. Deadline's headline saved its harshest wording ("Needs A Body Bag") for Body of Lies, not Ember.

### Gaps
- No verbatim 2008 Variety, THR, LA Times, NYT or AP quote calling Ember a "flop," "bomb" or "disappointment" was found. Retrieving these requires Wayback access, which was blocked, or paywalled archives such as ProQuest or Variety's archive.
- Year-end 2008 "flops" roundups that might mention Ember were not found.
